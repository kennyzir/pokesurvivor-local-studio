PokeSurvivor Local Studio

A personal Windows tool for recording PokéSurvivor gameplay, making English video edits and scheduling uploads to the owner's YouTube channel.

Privacy policyTerms of useYouTube channel

How it works

The owner explicitly enables game-tab recording in Chrome. Completed recordings are saved locally. A local companion edits selected moments and uses Google OAuth to upload videos to the authorized YouTube channel. Scheduled videos become public at the owner's chosen time.

This is a personal-use tool. It has no hosted account database or separate cloud backend. It is an unofficial fan tool and is not affiliated with Nintendo, The Pokémon Company, Google or YouTube.

Privacy policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026.

Local recordings and files

The Chrome extension captures the explicitly selected supported game tab and its audio, when enabled. It saves per-run video, gameplay event logs and editing markers on the owner's computer. The companion reads the owner's configured recording folder. Original recordings and logs remain local until the owner chooses to share them. This tool does not automatically delete originals.

Google and YouTube access

The tool requests youtube.upload to upload videos and youtube.readonly to identify the authorized channel and check uploaded video status. It does not request Gmail, Drive or permission to manage unrelated Google services. Google OAuth tokens are stored locally, encrypted with Windows protection tied to the current Windows user. They are used to authenticate requests to Google's official endpoints.

Edited video, titles, descriptions, tags and publication settings are sent to YouTube according to the owner's configuration. YouTube processes and hosts that content under its own policies. The tool does not sell Google user data or share OAuth credentials with other recipients.

Optional model assistance

When the owner enables Codex-assisted editing, selected gameplay frames and a limited editing evidence summary are sent through the owner's existing ChatGPT-authenticated Codex session. Full original video files, complete gameplay logs and Google OAuth credentials are not sent to that planning session. Provider processing follows the account's settings and the provider's policies. Local editing can continue with a factual fallback when model assistance is unavailable.

Retention and control

Source media, local job records and credentials are retained until the owner removes them. The owner can stop recording, pause new local production jobs, revoke the application's access in Google Account settings, and remove its local credentials and files. Pausing the local companion does not cancel videos already scheduled on YouTube.

Limited use of Google data

PokeSurvivor Local Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google account information and OAuth credentials are not provided to the optional editing model, sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized models.
